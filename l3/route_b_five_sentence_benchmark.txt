# These are surface-pronunciation test strings, not a new orthography.
# Post-consonantal /r/ is written as r in lemfamre/mefamre so the benchmark
# follows Commune Elven allophony ([r] after a consonant) without changing
# the qce frontend implementation during this test.

S3|teɹa ta nemiɹd puld bufilje ne?|Will you (equal) cook the bread?
S4|atola milaa.|They (not visible) are flying.
S5|ren ta moɹan puld mefamre uɹd.|I did not take the fruit.
